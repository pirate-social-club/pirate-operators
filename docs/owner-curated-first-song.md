# Owner-curated first song

Successor, November 8: the completed registered journey below is unchanged,
including policy pirate-owner-curated/2026-11-05/1, its hashes and its
acceptance evidence. The owner-only unregistered successor for launch is
[owner-only MegaETH song publication](megaeth-song-publication.md).

The owner corrected the active release scope on November 5: automated content
moderation is deferred. Creator-wallet ownership, source recognition, ROOT
registration and publication remain required. The existing managed community
remains independent of c22. This is one bounded owner release, not general
community posting or a deployment of the deferred multi-creator successor.

The normative policy is [owner-curated-policy.json](owner-curated-policy.json).
Its canonical SHA-256 is exported by packages/community/owner-policy.ts. A new
directory configuration revision must commit to that policy. Revision 1 and
its automated policy remain historical evidence; their meaning never changes.
Readers accept both exact policy pairs and require a post to match the policy
of its actual configuration revision. Owner-curated posts use
pirate.community-post/2 and explicitly identify rating and language as operator
declarations, with automated content moderation deferred.

The submitted master, lyric manifest, post and author identities remain fixed.
The original job input hash and authority, held operation, provider receipts,
request quotas and settlements are retained. An operator-signed, append-only
amendment binds those originals to the successor configuration, declaration,
creator recipient and finite execution grant. Its separate admission operation
cannot replace the held operation. Adopting an amendment rejects unknown
provider sends and any predecessor pin, registration or publication intent.
New-master and publication writes enforce the captured owner authority.
Private stem attachments require the authenticated account, same-origin CSRF
protection and ownership of that exact submission. They can finish before policy
adoption or funding readiness. Publication still requires both seals and the
controller-signed exact-job amendment.

The owner policy accepts genuine retained ACRCloud recognition for at most
24 hours from its original provider acquisition. Signing under the new policy
does not move that timestamp. This explicit policy interval differs from the
old automated policy's one-hour interval. Identified audio blocks publication;
inconclusive audio remains held. An absent, stale, altered or untrusted receipt
cannot become a successful check. No text moderation or classifier result is
created by the adoption, and no additional content-provider request is needed.

The successor keeps the original aggregate allowance: five song artifacts,
one ROOT registration to the captured creator wallet and one MegaETH post,
with the same periods and fee limits. The old allowance cannot remain spendable
under the successor configuration. The configuration pin and directory update
are separately bounded setup operations. Existing payment and learning-record
contracts stay disabled; no reward payment is authorized here.

The composer requires the recording, instrumental and vocal MP3s. Stems are
private fixed-slot attachments with their own checksum, lease and seal. A lost
R2 response is reconciled against the retained object before another upload.
Neither attachment changes the master or the sealed publication input.
Browser files survive reload in IndexedDB; authentication remains in the host
cookie. Existing submitted recordings can attach their missing stems without
starting a second release. Public pinning requires both retained seals.

Forced alignment consumes the vocal stem and accepted lyric text after
publication, following the centralized boundary documented in
[song-stems-source-provenance.json](song-stems-source-provenance.json). It is a
supplemental timing artifact, never a replacement lyric manifest. The independently reviewed alignment foundation and private vocal-read helper
remain offline source. No timing route, migration or ElevenLabs call is enabled;
character-only response fallback remains deferred.

Local tests cover the actual D1/R2 handlers and a simulated provider/chain journey
from the retained classifier refusal through the owner-curated publication.
They preserve old receipts and quotas, reject changed declarations and prior
public work, and reconstruct the versioned post. Browser tests cover saved
recordings and both stems after interruption and reload. The separate [live acceptance record](deployments/first-song-managed-20261105.first-song-acceptance.json)
records actual stems, provider pins, ROOT registration, MegaETH publication and
fresh anonymous browser playback after reload. The same job is published; its
original held admission and failed operation remain intact as historical evidence.
The selected recording's sealed title is "Test". This qualifies the bounded
owner-curated testnet journey, with the stated moderation and alignment limits.
